Garlic Butter Chicken

Home

A simple and flavorful chicken dish made with garlic, butter, and herbs. Perfect for a quick weeknight dinner.

Ingredients

Serves 2–3

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 teaspoon dried herbs
  • 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Season.

    Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and herbs.

  2. Cook.

    Melt butter in a pan over medium heat and cook chicken for 6–7 minutes per side.

  3. Add Garlic.

    Add minced garlic in the last 2 minutes of cooking and spoon butter over the chicken.

  4. Finish With Lemon.

    slice the lemon and add it to the pan for a minute before serving.
