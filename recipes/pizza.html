If you’re a home cook eager to make the perfect pizza dough, we’ve got your back. You might be thinking, “It’s just pizza dough, how hard could it be?” But the truth is, making great pizza dough really is a bit of an art. Too often, homemade pizza ends up tasting bland or feels more like bread than a proper pizza base. With the right technique and a bit of patience, you can create a dough that bakes up with the flavor and texture of a restaurant-style crust that will truly blow your mind.

The key to perfect pizza dough is time. You’ve got to let it rest! This recipe does take some time, but every minute is worth it. A long, slow rise develops flavor and gives the dough that light, airy texture you see in pizzeria pizzas. I used to rush through my dough and was always disappointed, but once I committed to the resting and rising process, everything changed. Follow these steps, give the dough the time it deserves, and you’ll be rewarded with a delicious foundation for your favorite homemade pizza creations.

Ingredients

3x 330-gram dough balls or 4x 250-gram

  • 370 grams lukewarm water
  • 1 heaping teaspoon active dry yeast
  • 600 grams "00" flour
  • 18 grams sea salt

Instructions

  1. Dissolve.

    In a bowl or stand mixer bowl, add the warm water and yeast.

    Stir until dissolved.

  2. Mix.

    Combine the flour and salt together in a separate bowl.

    Gradually add the flour and salt mix into the wet ingredients until a dough forms.

    • Using a stand mixer? Mix on low using a dough hook for 5–10 minutes, until firm, smooth and elastic.
    • Mixing by hand? Stir with a wooden spoon, then knead on a floured surface for 10 minutes.
  3. Rest.

    Cover the bowl with a dish towel and let the dough rest and rise for 2 hours, or until it's doubled in size.

  4. Divide.

    Once the dough has risen, divide it into 3 or 4 equal pieces, depending on the size of pizza you're making.

    • If divided into 3 pieces: makes 16-inch (40 cm) pizzas.
    • If divided into 4 pieces: makes 12-inch (30 cm) pizzas.

    Roll the dough balls until perfectly rounded (this helps keep the base circular during stretching).

    Place each ball in a separate bowl or tray, cover with a dish towel and leave to rise in a warm place for 30–60 minutes, or until doubled in size.

    If freezing for later use, place each dough ball in an individual bag and freeze at this point.

  5. Stretch.

    Place a risen dough ball on a lightly floured surface. Flour your hands and press the dough into a small, flat disc using your fingertips.

    Working from the center, push the dough outwards while spreading your fingers to make the disc larger.

    Pick up the disc and gently stretch around the edge, allowing gravity to pull it into a circle.

    Neapolitan-style pizza bases should be very thin you should be able to see light through the base.

  6. Place on a pizza peel.
